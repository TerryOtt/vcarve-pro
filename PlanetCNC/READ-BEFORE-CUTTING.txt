PURDUE COASTER - SIX DIRECT-GENERATED PLANETCNC TAP PROGRAMS

These are independent CAM programs, not exports from the VCarve job you previewed.
Use the six files together as a matched set. Do not mix either half with earlier
VCarve or other plug/pocket programs.

STOCK AND ORIGINS
  Maple: 4 x 4 x 0.5 inches, pocket stock.
  Walnut: 4 x 4 x 0.5 inches, plug stock.
  Logo: approximately 3 inches wide, centered. Registered mark removed.
  XY origin: spindle/tool center at the lower-left stock corner.
  Z origin: actual tip referenced to the ORIGINAL, flat stock top.
  The files retain your active work coordinate system. They do not establish
  offsets, home axes, change tools, or probe automatically.
  Each new stock mounting needs its own correct XY/Z setup.

INLAY DIMENSIONS
  Maple maximum pocket depth: 0.125 inch.
  Nominal full-depth plug insertion: 0.120 inch.
  Nominal bottom glue gap at full pocket depth: 0.005 inch.
  Surface/backing clearance: 0.050 inch.
  Walnut maximum background cut: 0.170 inch.
  Walnut backing below its cleared background: 0.330 inch.
  Male side fit allowance: 0.0005 inch inward, a small deliberate clearance.
  Narrow portions of the tapered design have shallower features.

CUT ORDER - COMPLETE ONE STOCK BEFORE MOUNTING THE OTHER
  Maple_01_quarter.tap  - 1/4 inch end mill
  Maple_02_eighth.tap   - 1/8 inch end mill
  Maple_03_V60.tap      - pointed 60 degree V-bit
  Walnut_01_quarter.tap - 1/4 inch end mill
  Walnut_02_eighth.tap  - 1/8 inch end mill
  Walnut_03_V60.tap     - SAME pointed 60 degree V-bit

PROGRAMMED TRIAL CUTTING SETTINGS
  All bits: S18000, nominal 18,000 RPM.
  Quarter end mill: feed 80 IPM, plunge 15 IPM, layer increments <=0.040 inch,
    raster spacing 0.100 inch. Ramp feed <=20 IPM, approximately 2 degree ramps.
  Eighth end mill: feed 50 IPM, plunge 10 IPM, layer increments <=0.020 inch,
    raster spacing 0.0375 inch. Ramp feed <=20 IPM, approximately 2 degree ramps.
  V-bit: feed 50 IPM, plunge 15 IPM, rough contour increments 0.005 inch.
    Finishing uses the simulated already-cut cone volume, with incremental
    additional cone penetration limited to 0.0125 inch per finishing pass.
  Very small end-mill loci without room for a ramp use slow 0.005 inch Z steps.
  These settings are generic trial starting values. They are not manufacturer
  specifications and do not certify bit strength. Your generic 60 degree bit
  is NOT treated as an Amana bit. Its package label reads 60 Degree 3F,
  1/4 x 2-1/2 x 60 degrees, code CXZ20260121; manufacturer is unidentified.
  If router RPM is set manually, the S command does not set the physical dial.

V-BIT GEOMETRY TO CHECK BEFORE A CUT
  These programs assume a sharp POINTED 60 degree INCLUDED-angle cutter.
  They do not compensate a flat, round, worn, or chipped tip.
  Its usable tapered cutting edges must extend at least 0.170 inch above the
  tip. Shank size and overall length do not establish usable cutting height.
  The cutting cone needs about 0.197 inch diameter at the deepest plug cut.

WORKHOLDING AND MOVES
  Secure the stock on a suitable spoilboard; keep fixtures, clamps and screws
  outside the complete machining and spindle/collet travel area.
  Clamp at the front/back stock margins or use an appropriate fixture; do not
  place a clamp over the logo or the plug's surrounding clearance band.
  Rapid transfers use Z=1.000 inch ABOVE the original stock top. Confirm that
  this clears the clamps and remains within the machine's available Z travel.
  Neighboring V-bit finishing paths also use short feed links inside the
  machining area. They follow already-cleared volume or pass 0.025 inch above
  the original top. Workholding must stay out of these links too.
  Between manual bit changes, retain XY if the stock has not moved. Establish
  the replacement bit's Z reference using the untouched original top plane or
  your correct tool-measuring routine, never the cleared pocket floor.

PREVIEW AND FIRST TEST
  Open each .tap file using PlanetCNC File > Open. G20 selects inches; G90
  selects absolute coordinates. Programs use ordinary G0/G1 moves and M3/M5
  spindle commands, with a 3 second start delay; each ends retracted with M30.
  Confirm the displayed size, origin, depth, cutter, workholding and machine
  behavior before running a test pair. The programs do not contain tool-change
  or automatic tool-measurement macros from your machine's VCarve postprocessor.
  Run the matched six-file set on a scrap/test pair first and check actual fit.
  Toolpath-3D-Preview.png shows the independently simulated cuts.
  Validation-Report.json records geometric checks and their resolution/limits.
  Geometry simulation does not model bit stress, runout, actual angle error,
  machine repeatability, wood grain, board flatness, or clamping.

TRIM THE WALNUT PLUG BEFORE FITTING
  The walnut program clears a rectangular band around the raised design.
  It leaves an uncut outer rim to retain workholding; that rim will prevent
  insertion if left attached. After all walnut cuts are complete, saw off the
  outer rim THROUGH THE CLEARED BAND, keeping the backing and all logo islands.
  Suggested rectangular trim lines, measured from the walnut XY origin:
    X approximately 0.375 and 3.625 inches;
    Y approximately 1.072 and 2.928 inches.
  Check the actual raised design and saw kerf before trimming. Do not saw into
  the raised tapered logo. Perimeter profiling/through-cuts are not in these files.

ASSEMBLY
  Flip the walnut's CNC-cut face down into the maple pocket and align the P.
  Test fit before glue. Apply the appropriate wood glue, clamp, and allow its
  specified cure time. Remove the remaining walnut backing and finish flush
  using a separate, correctly set up removal operation. Those assembly/backing
  removal operations are not contained in these six programs.
