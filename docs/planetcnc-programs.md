# Experimental PlanetCNC programs

The six `.tap` files in `PlanetCNC/` were generated by `coaster/generate_tap.py`, independently of VCarve. They are a matched experimental set, not exports of the user's VCarve preview. Do not mix their pocket or plug halves with another calculation.

## Cut sequence

| Stock | First | Second | Third |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maple pocket | Maple_01_quarter.tap | Maple_02_eighth.tap | Maple_03_V60.tap |
| Walnut plug | Walnut_01_quarter.tap | Walnut_02_eighth.tap | Walnut_03_V60.tap |

Complete one mounted stock before switching to the other. Tool changes are manual. Programs retain the active work coordinate system and do not automatically home, probe, establish offsets, or change tools.

## Programmed assumptions

- Both stocks: 4 × 4 × 0.5 inches; XY at lower-left, Z at original top.
- Pocket maximum depth: 0.125 inches; nominal plug insertion: 0.120 inches.
- Glue gap: 0.005 inches; surface gap: 0.050 inches; plug background maximum depth: 0.170 inches.
- Male side clearance: 0.0005 inches inward.
- Pointed 60° included-angle cutter; no flat/rounded-tip compensation. Required usable tapered cutting height is at least 0.170 inches.
- Rapid clearance: 1 inch above original top. Some short finishing links traverse cleared volume or run 0.025 inches above the original top.
- G20 inches, G90 absolute, G0/G1 motion, M3/M5 spindle, three-second start delay, M30 end.

| Cutter | RPM | Feed IPM | Plunge IPM | Layer/increment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|

| 1/8-inch endmill | 18,000 | 50 | 10 | ≤ 0.020 inches |
| Pointed 60° V-bit | 18,000 | 50 | 15 | 0.005-inch rough increments; finishing additional cone penetration ≤ 0.0125 inches |

These are generic trial settings, not verified manufacturer recommendations or stress limits. An S command does not adjust a manually controlled router dial.

## Verification and limits

`Validation-Report.json` records swept-tool numerical checks at a 0.004-inch grid resolution. The saved report shows maximum overcut about 0.000060 inches for maple and 0.000034 inches for walnut, maximum residual about 0.000790/0.000837 inches, and no sampled interference exceeding 0.001 inches. These are sampled simulation results, not proof of physical fit.

`Toolpath-Simulation.png` and `Toolpath-3D-Preview.png` illustrate the independent simulation. The source simulation depth array is a regenerable local artifact excluded from Git. No machine run, actual test fit, or VCarve calculation of this independent set has been performed. Simulation does not model bit stress, runout, angle error, grain, machine accuracy, or fixtures.

Before any trial run, review all six files in the actual PlanetCNC installation, confirm the physical bit geometry, clearances and coordinate system, and use a scrap matched pair. See `PlanetCNC/READ-BEFORE-CUTTING.txt` for full operating assumptions.

The plug retains an outer workholding rim. It must be trimmed through the cleared band before insertion. Glue/clamp/cure and walnut backing removal are separate operations; backing removal and coaster perimeter profiling are not in these six files.
